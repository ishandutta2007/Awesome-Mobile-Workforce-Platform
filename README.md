# Awesome-Mobile-Workforce-Platform

## Top Mobile Workforce Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Field Service Management, Technician Dispatch, Mobile Work Orders, Scheduling & On-Site Operations*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Mobile Workforce / Field Service Management**. These systems help organizations schedule, dispatch, and support mobile technicians and field workers with work orders, routing, parts, customer information, and offline-capable mobile apps.



**Examples** include Skedulo, Workiz, Zuper, ServiceMax, Oracle Field Service, Salesforce Field Service, ClickSoftware, FieldAware, ReachOut, and FieldEZ (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full-featured mobile workforce platforms are mostly commercial. Practical open options exist through **Odoo OCA Field Service**, **ERPNext**, emerging projects such as **OpenFieldService** and **FieldOpt**, and related scheduling/roster tools. This section lists the strongest available open resources and is realistic about the gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Skedulo](https://www.skedulo.com/)**  

  Mobile workforce management and scheduling platform focused on intelligent dispatch and field team optimization.



- **[Workiz](https://www.workiz.com/)**  

  Field service management software popular with home-service businesses for scheduling, dispatch, invoicing, and customer communication.



- **[Zuper](https://www.zuper.co/)**  

  Field service and mobile workforce platform offering work order management, scheduling, and technician mobile apps.



- **[ServiceMax](https://www.servicemax.com/)**  

  Enterprise field service management solution strong in asset-centric industries (manufacturing, medical devices, energy) with parts and IoT capabilities.



- **[Oracle Field Service](https://www.oracle.com/cx/field-service/)**  

  Enterprise field service cloud from Oracle covering scheduling, routing, mobile workforce management, and collaboration.



- **[Salesforce Field Service](https://www.salesforce.com/products/field-service/)**  

  Field service capabilities deeply integrated with Salesforce CRM, including intelligent scheduling and a robust offline mobile app.



- **[ClickSoftware (Salesforce)](https://www.salesforce.com/)**  

  Historically a leading field service optimization suite; now part of the broader Salesforce Field Service ecosystem.



- **[FieldAware](https://www.fieldaware.com/)**  

  Cloud field service management platform for work orders, scheduling, inventory, and mobile technician enablement.



- **[ReachOut](https://www.reachoutsuite.com/)**  

  Field service and mobile workforce solution focused on scheduling, dispatch, and real-time field visibility.



- **[FieldEZ](https://www.fieldez.com/)**  

  Mobile workforce and field force automation platform used for sales and service teams in various markets.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Odoo OCA Field Service](https://github.com/OCA/field-service)**  

  The strongest open-source field-service module set for Odoo—covering field service orders, routes, skills, vehicles, recurring service, and stock integration.



- **[ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  

  Full open-source ERP with CRM, maintenance, support, inventory, and project features that can be configured for field service workflows.



- **[OpenFieldService](https://github.com/clawnify/OpenFieldService)**  

  Open-source field service scheduling and dispatch system positioned as a self-hosted alternative for pest control, HVAC, plumbing, and similar trades.



- **[FieldOpt](https://github.com/zblauser/fieldopt)**  

  Open-source web-based job organization and field management tool with technician assignment, skill matching, and basic routing concepts.



- **[DutyDock and open rostering tools](https://github.com/dutydock/dutydock)**  

  Open-source shift planning and rostering software for teams with complex scheduling constraints (useful adjacent capability).



- **[Mobile workforce attendance / GPS open apps](https://github.com/)**  

  Community PWA and mobile projects for GPS-verified punch-in/out, live team tracking, and offline field attendance.



- **[Work-order and ticket open trackers](https://github.com/)**  

  Lightweight open systems for creating, assigning, and closing field work orders.



- **[Route optimization open libraries (VROOM, OR-Tools)](https://github.com/)**  

  Open routing engines that can be integrated into custom dispatch solutions for technician routes.



- **[Inventory and parts open modules](https://github.com/)**  

  Open inventory components (often from ERPNext/Odoo) used to track van stock and spare parts for field teams.



- **[Customer signature and form open collectors](https://github.com/)**  

  Mobile-friendly open tools for capturing signatures, checklists, and job completion data offline.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Building on **Odoo Community + OCA Field Service** when you need real FSM workflows connected to CRM, inventory, and accounting.

- Using **ERPNext** for broader ERP + service operations in a single open stack.

- Evaluating **OpenFieldService** or **FieldOpt** for lighter, self-hosted scheduling and dispatch needs.

- Combining open route optimizers with a simple work-order system for custom mobile workforce solutions.

- Accepting that polished offline mobile apps, AI-assisted scheduling, large-scale optimization, parts logistics, and enterprise support still favor commercial platforms (Salesforce Field Service, ServiceMax, Oracle Field Service, Skedulo, Workiz, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on data ownership, customization, and cost control for smaller field teams or specific verticals.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Manage customers and assets in Odoo/ERPNext → create and schedule field service orders → optimize routes with open solvers → equip technicians with a mobile/PWA work-order app → sync completions and parts usage back to the ERP. Suitable for organizations with development capacity. Most mid-market and enterprise field service operations continue to adopt commercial mobile workforce platforms for reliability and mobile experience.
