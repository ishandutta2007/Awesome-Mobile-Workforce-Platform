## 💡 Overview & Market Insights 📈

The **Mobile Workforce Management (MWM)** and **Field Service Management (FSM)** sectors represent a combined estimated market size of **~$13.2 Billion to $14.8 Billion** (MWM estimated at ~$8.1B and FSM at ~$6.1B–$6.7B with a ~12–13% CAGR).

> [!NOTE]
> **Market Dynamics:** The sector is **moderately to highly fragmented**. While enterprise giants like Salesforce, Oracle, and ServiceMax capture large enterprise accounts, numerous specialized SaaS providers and open-source solutions flourish across niche verticals (HVAC, plumbing, telecommunications, utilities, and medical equipment maintenance).
